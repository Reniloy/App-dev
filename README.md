## Highlights & Details

### 1. Suits
* **Network:** USA Network
* **Why it stands out:** Sharp dialogue, corporate strategy, and the dynamic between a top closer and a photographic-memory associate.
* **More Info:** [Suits on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1632701/)

> "I don't play the odds, I play the man." — *Harvey Specter*

### 2. Peaky Blinders
* **Setting:** Birmingham, England (`1919–1930s`)
* **Why it stands out:** Gritty cinematography, period soundtrack, and Tommy Shelby's calculated rise to power.
* **More Info:** [Peaky Blinders on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2442560/)

> "By order of the Peaky Blinders." — *Arthur Shelby*

### 3. The Mentalist
* **Focus:** California Bureau of Investigation (`CBI`)
* **Why it stands out:** Patrick Jane's cold-reading skills, attention to micro-expressions, and long-running cat-and-mouse chase.
* **More Info:** [The Mentalist on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1196946/)
